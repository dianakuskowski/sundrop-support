Sundrop

Support

Sundrop is a private companion app for a small group's Walt Disney World trip — a shared itinerary, a planning checklist, and live updates. It isn't a public product; it's shared only with people invited to that trip.

Getting help

If something isn't working, or you have a question about using the app, email dianatkach@mac.com.

I forgot the passcode

The passcode was given to you by the trip organizer — reach out to them directly, or email the address above.

Deleting your data

Trip content (itinerary, checklist items, updates) is shared with everyone on the trip via iCloud and can be edited or removed from within the app. To have your account's data removed entirely, email the address above.

Privacy Policy
