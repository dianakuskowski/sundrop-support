Privacy Policy

Sundrop · Last updated October 25, 2026

Sundrop is a free, non-commercial trip planner for a small group traveling together. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts of its own.

Where your trip lives

A trip is stored in the trip organizer's iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit, and shared only with the people the organizer invites. Nobody without an invitation can see it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Without an invitation, the app shows only a sample trip, which stays on your phone.

What's in a trip

  • The plan: the trip's details (its name, dates and who's going), each day's itinerary, reservations and notes, the planning checklist, and updates the organizer posts.
  • What the group marks: rides checked off and park-guide picks, each with the name the person typed and when.
  • Trip settings: for example a child's height, used to show which rides they can go on.
  • Who's invited: iCloud shows the organizer each invited person's name and whether they've joined. The app also uses an identifier Apple gives it for your iCloud account (not your name or email) to tell the organizer's phone from everyone else's.

Everyone in the trip can see all of this.

Notifications

When the organizer posts an update, Apple delivers a banner to everyone in the trip. The only thing stored outside the trip to make that happen is the time of the latest post. The banner's text is filled in on your phone from the trip itself.

Optional AI features

The organizer can use AI-assisted editing with their own Anthropic API key, stored only in that device's Keychain. When they do, the text they submit and the trip days it edits go directly from their device to Anthropic's API; see Anthropic's privacy policy. Nobody else's device uses it.

Backups

On the organizer's iPhone, the app saves a copy of the trip once a day to the organizer's own private iCloud storage, keeping the last 30 days, so it can be recovered if something is deleted by mistake.

Deleting your data

  • Anyone in the trip can choose Leave this trip in the app's Settings tab. It removes the trip from your phone and your iCloud.
  • Names you typed next to check-offs stay with the trip; ask the organizer to remove them.
  • The organizer can remove anyone from the trip, or stop sharing it.
  • Copies in the organizer's daily backups expire after 30 days.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data, and we (the developer) don't receive it.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking software.
