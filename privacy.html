Privacy Policy

Sundrop · Last updated July 29, 2026

Sundrop is a private, non-commercial app built for one small group's trip. It is not a public product, doesn't run ads, and doesn't include any analytics or tracking software.

What the app stores

  • Trip content — the itinerary, planning checklist, and posted updates. This is stored in a shared iCloud (CloudKit) database visible to everyone with the app, so the group can see the same live plan.
  • Names you type — e.g. the name you enter once so the app can show who completed a checklist item or posted an update. This is stored alongside that content in the same shared database and is visible to the rest of the group.
  • Your iCloud account identifier — used only to determine whether your device is allowed to make itinerary edits. Apple does not share your name or email address with the app.

Optional AI features

Some features let a user enter their own Anthropic API key to enable AI-assisted editing. That key is stored only in your device's Keychain — it is never sent to us or stored in the shared database. When you use an AI feature, the text you submit is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's API to process your request; see Anthropic's privacy policy for how they handle it. This is entirely optional — the rest of the app works without it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs beyond Apple's CloudKit and, only if you choose to use it, Anthropic's API.
  • We don't collect data from anyone outside the small group this app was built for.

Deleting your data

Checklist items and updates can be deleted directly in the app. To have any other data associated with your device removed, email dianatkach@mac.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: dianatkach@mac.com.
