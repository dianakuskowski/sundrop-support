'Ohana Run Club

Support

'Ohana Run Club helps a small group of friends plan runs together: who's going, where to meet, whether it's a day for outside, and who's running late.

Getting started

  • Start a club on the first screen, then invite friends from the Club tab. They need the app and an iCloud account.
  • Joined by invitation? Tap the invite link on your iPhone and the club opens in the app.

Leaving or deleting

In the Club tab, members can leave and delete their data. The owner can delete the club for everyone.
